Estoiko Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Estoiko is an Android app developed by MoonDevs. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it is stored and how you can delete it.

Summary

  • Estoiko has no user accounts, does not use the internet and shows no ads.
  • Your tasks are stored only on your phone.
  • MoonDevs does not collect, sell or share your data with anyone.

What data the app uses

Estoiko only uses what you type into it:

  • Your tasks: name, description, type (normal or priority), time, start date and repetition.
  • The subtasks of each task.
  • Which tasks and subtasks you marked as done, and on which day.

This data is used only to show you your task list. The app does not request any special phone permissions (no internet, location, contacts or camera) and includes no third-party analytics or advertising tools.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it and MoonDevs has no access to it.

Android backup

If Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android may save a copy of Estoiko's data in a private folder of your own Google Drive account, so you can get your tasks back if you change phones or reinstall the app. Estoiko only allows this backup when it can be end-to-end encrypted with your phone's screen lock (PIN, pattern or password); if it cannot be encrypted, it is not made. Android may also move the data directly to a new phone when you transfer your apps.

This backup is made and managed by Google, under its own terms. MoonDevs cannot see or read it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete a task in the app (open it and tap "Delete").
  • Delete all of Estoiko's data from your phone's settings: Apps › Estoiko › Storage › Clear data (names may vary by phone).
  • Uninstall the app, which deletes its data from the phone.

The Android backup, if there is one, is managed from your phone's and your Google Account's backup settings.

Children

Estoiko is intended for people aged 13 and over. The app does not collect data from anyone, including minors.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with its update date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, write to us at estoikobuddy@gmail.com.
